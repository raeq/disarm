- **A payload encoded in ordinary text is named on the pages that send a reader to `llm_guardrail` (#1208).**
  Base64, hex, binary, ROT-n, Morse and reversal are plain ASCII to every preset, which neither decodes nor
  reports them. `llm_guardrail` folds case, which happens to corrupt base64, and the pages now call that a
  side effect and not a control. `THREAT_MODEL.md` no longer says disarm does not decode percent-encoding,
  which `decode_smuggled` has read since #727. The docstrings of `detect_encoding`, `decode_to_utf8` and
  `decode_smuggled` say what they answer. #729's open question is decided: `decode_smuggled` does not read
  base64, hex or binary, and they stay out of `inspect_anomalies`.
